It is a sort of rite of passage for New Hampshirites to hike all 48 4,000-foot peaks in the White Mountains before they die. My New Hampshirite partner has completed 47, but plans to run all 48 in one shot when he returns to the state.

The topography heatmap of the Whites is covered over three different needlepoints, each hand-painted and embroidered over a four-year period. Custom-built white oak frames, using leftover material from the previous project, display the needlepoints.
